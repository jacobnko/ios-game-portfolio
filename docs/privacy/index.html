Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile games published by jacobkostudio (bundle identifiers under com.jacobkostudio.*), including but not limited to Chordline and the other titles in this portfolio. Every game shares the same underlying technology, so this one policy covers all of them; the specific app you are using is named in its own App Store listing.

What we collect, and why

SourceDataPurpose
Game progressWhich stages you've cleared, scores, stars, time playedSo your progress carries across your devices, via your own iCloud account
Advertising (Google AdMob)Advertising identifier (if you allow tracking), device type, approximate locationShowing and measuring ads
Analytics (Firebase Analytics)In-game events (e.g. which stage you started or cleared, how long an attempt took) and an anonymous install identifierUnderstanding which parts of the game are too easy, too hard, or confusing
Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)Crash stack traces and the game state at the timeFixing bugs
Purchases (Apple StoreKit)Purchase and restore recordsUnlocking what you paid for (e.g. removing ads)

None of this includes your name, email address, or any way to identify you personally. Progress data is stored in your own private iCloud account — we do not have access to it, and it is never linked to advertising or analytics data.

Advertising and tracking

Ads are served by Google AdMob. The first time you open the game, iOS will ask whether you allow the app to track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, you still see ads — they are simply not personalized to you. You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

  • Turn off analytics/crash reporting in the app's own Settings screen.
  • Change or withdraw ad tracking permission in your device's system settings at any time.
  • Request deletion of your progress by using "Reset Progress" in the app, or by contacting us below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the update.

Contact

Questions about this policy: jacobkosmart@gmail.com

광고 및 추적

광고는 Google AdMob이 게재합니다. 앱을 처음 열면 iOS가 다른 회사의 앱·웹사이트에서 사용자를 추적하는 것을 허용할지 물어봅니다(앱 추적 투명성). 거부해도 광고는 계속 나오며, 다만 맞춤 광고가 아닌 일반 광고가 나옵니다. 이 설정은 기기의 설정 → 개인정보 보호 및 보안 → 추적에서 언제든 바꿀 수 있습니다.

어린이 관련

이 게임들은 13세 미만 어린이를 대상으로 만들어지지 않았으며, 13세 미만으로부터 고의로 개인정보를 수집하지 않습니다.

선택할 수 있는 것

  • 앱 내 설정 화면에서 분석·충돌 보고를 끌 수 있습니다.
  • 기기 시스템 설정에서 광고 추적 권한을 언제든 바꿀 수 있습니다.
  • 앱의 "진행도 초기화" 기능을 쓰거나, 아래 연락처로 요청하면 진행도 삭제를 요청할 수 있습니다.
